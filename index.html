CALL SCREENING & PHONE FEATURES 12.1 Android CallScreeningService Our app uses Android's official CallScreeningService API. Here is exactly what happens during call screening: text Step 1: Incoming call arrives at your phone Step 2: Android OS notifies our CallScreeningService Step 3: We extract ONLY the phone number Step 4: We check: Is it in VIP list? → Allow Step 5: We check: Is vacation mode active? → Block (if not VIP) Step 6: We check: Is it business hours? → Allow/Block Step 7: Decision is made locally, instantly Step 8: If blocked: Number + timestamp saved to inbox Step 9: NOTHING is sent to any external service 12.2 What Happens When a Call is Blocked The call is silently rejected (caller gets standard busy/voicemail) The number and timestamp are saved to your local inbox A notification appears on your device The caller is NOT notified that they were specifically blocked by an app 12.3 What Callers Experience When their call is blocked: They may hear a busy signal or be sent to voicemail They are NOT told the call was blocked by an app They can still leave a voicemail (standard behavior) They can call back after your blocking period ends 13.
